# Job Description — Working Student in AI Engineering

```
Your mission

As a Working Student in AI Engineering, you support the development and operation of AI-powered products for the finance industry.

You work at the intersection of software, data, and AI — evaluating model performance, building automations, and contributing to scalable solutions for real-world use cases while gaining hands-on experience with modern AI technologies.

Evaluation & Data
- Build and run evaluation pipelines for LLM-based systems
- Analyze metrics, identify edge cases, and track quality
- Create and maintain Golden and Silver datasets
- Work with evaluation frameworks and tools (like Langfuse)

Engineering & Automation
- Develop Python scripts for AI workflows (APIs, data processing, monitoring)
- Support maintenance and improvement of existing systems

Collaboration
- Work closely with engineering teams to improve system quality
- Contribute proactively to technical challenges

Your profile

What You Bring
- Enrolled student in Computer Science, Data Science, or similar
- Solid Python skills beyond notebooks
- Experience with REST APIs (JSON, authentication)
- Basic ML understanding
- Git workflow experience
- Ability to critically evaluate AI outputs (both code and data)
- Problem-solving mindset
- Very good English knowledge, knowledge of German is an advantage

Nice to Have
- Experience with LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic)
- Vector databases (e.g. Qdrant)
- AI coding assistants

Our offer
- Hands-on experience with modern AI systems
- Real impact on production use cases
- Exciting challenge in an open-minded, international team
- Good development opportunities and possibility of long-term cooperation even after graduation
- Flat hierarchies and short communication channels
- Attractive remuneration: 16 €/h
- Modern workplace in the heart of Munich with view of the Theresienwiese
- Hybrid working possible (office and home office alternately)
- Free drinks and fruit as well as an always well-filled ice box in summer
```
