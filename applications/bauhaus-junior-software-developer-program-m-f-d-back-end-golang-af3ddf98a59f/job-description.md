# Job Description — Junior Software Developer

```
Better digital. The Digital Commerce division represents a connected BAUHAUS of the future through digitalization.

Your role:
As a Junior Software Developer (m/f/d) in our Fast-Track-Program, we will train you for 18 months in various teams within Digital Commerce to become an experienced developer (m/f/d) with a lot of practical experience
In the first semester, the focus is on back-end development in our Team Checkout or Marketplace Platform – in the following semesters, you will expand your knowledge in additional frameworks
You will gradually grow into your role, take on increasingly challenging tasks, and support exciting projects in an agile environment
One day a week is dedicated to developing your technical skills through online courses, cloud technologies (GCP), and agile working methods (e.g., Scrum, OKR)
After completing the Fast-Track-Program, we look forward to integrating you as a team member in one of our 25 cross-functional product teams

Your profile:
Successfully completed studies in Business Informatics or Computer Science
Initial practical programming experience as a Junior Software Developer (m/f/d) with Go
You are eager to learn additional programming languages and frameworks
Strong enthusiasm for your own development
Passion for creative, elegant solutions and the ability to independently deliver testable, maintainable, and high-quality code
You are a communicative team player and appreciate an international working environment

Our offer:
Flexibility for a good work-life balance: possibility of working remotely, flexible working hours, 30 days paid vacation with up to 12 additional unpaid vacation days
State-of-the-art working environment on the BAUHAUS campus
Permanent employment contract and attractive remuneration
Employee benefits: shopping discounts, corporate benefits, company pension plan
Technology & innovation with state-of-the-art technologies
```
