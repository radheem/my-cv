Working Student (Werkstudent) — BMW Group

BMW Group offers working-student positions across software, data, cloud, and AI/ML for students
enrolled at a university. Typical expectations for such roles:

- Enrolled student (Bachelor's or Master's) for the duration of the role.
- ~20 hours/week during the lecture period, with more possible during semester breaks.
- A relevant field of study (computer science, software/data engineering, or similar).
- Hands-on contribution to real engineering work alongside the team — software development,
  data pipelines, cloud/Kubernetes platforms, and/or ML tooling, depending on the team.
- Good English; German is helpful and a plus for many teams.
- Locations across BMW Group sites in Germany; hybrid arrangements are common.

The role is an opportunity to apply academic knowledge to production systems at scale, learn
BMW's engineering practices, and grow within a large, modern software and data organisation.
