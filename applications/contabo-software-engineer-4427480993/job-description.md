Your creative field

We are looking for a full-time, permanent Software Engineer (all genders) to start as soon as possible.

We operate remote-first within Germany, but if you live close to one of our locations (Munich, Cologne, Hamburg or Berlin), you are free to choose whether you work hybrid or fully on-site.

As a Software Engineer in our team, you will contribute to the development of new features based on clearly defined tasks and requirements.

You will work on real product challenges and help shape solutions that deliver tangible value to our customers. Over time, you will build a deep understanding of our system architecture and business domain, enabling you to make sound technical decisions in your daily work.

You actively follow architectural guidelines and take responsibility for writing high-quality unit and integration tests to ensure the stability, reliability, and long-term maintainability of our software.

Code quality is important to us, and you will play a key role in upholding best practices across different areas such as SQL databases, web services, CI/CD pipelines and message-driven systems.

You will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including product, QA and other developers, to translate new ideas and requirements into practical, well-designed technical solutions.

Part of your role will also include supporting performance improvements, identifying technical weaknesses, and contributing to testing efforts to help us continuously improve our systems and processes.

If you are looking for a role where you can grow your skills, take ownership and be part of a supportive and collaborative team culture, we would love to hear from you.
