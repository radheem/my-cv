\opening{Dear Hiring Team,}

I build the unglamorous parts of AI that make it dependable: the data pipelines that feed it, the platforms that run it, and the monitoring that keeps it honest in production. Your Senior Data Engineer / Data Scientist role describes exactly the work I gravitate toward, turning a live platform's data into insights people can act on, and I would be glad to do it for a HealthTech product used every day in real therapeutic settings.

For two years at Bluefin Exchange I was the design authority for the core services of a high-throughput crypto exchange, where reliability, scalability, and long-term maintainability were not nice-to-haves but the whole job. I owned systems in production end to end, built Kafka streaming pipelines and cloud-native services on Kubernetes, and thought constantly about what happens after the code ships. That production-first instinct is what your posting keeps returning to, and it is how I work by default.

The AI and data side is where I have spent my recent projects. I built Second Brain, a self-hosted document-intelligence service whose durable, retryable ETL pipeline extracts, chunks, embeds, and indexes documents for retrieval-augmented generation, with semantic search and local model inference. Alongside it, my O-RAN AIML Framework is an end-to-end ML platform on Kubernetes with Kubeflow training pipelines and KServe serving over a proper feature store and artifact storage; it was graded 1.0 (A) as a research project. Both are about the same thing your role needs: deploying, operating, and monitoring ML and Generative AI in production, not just building models in a notebook.

I am based in Ilmenau and currently doing my Master of Research at TU Ilmenau, available full-time now and happy with a hybrid setup in the Frankfurt Rhine-Main region; I can relocate within Germany in two to three weeks. I am Blue Card-ready, so there is no sponsorship overhead, only a contract to issue. I would welcome the chance to talk about how I can help evolve Findr's data platform.

\closing{Sincerely,}{Radheem Bin Razi}
