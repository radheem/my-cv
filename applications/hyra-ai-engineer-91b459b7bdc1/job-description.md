# Job Description — Full-Stack Engineer (AI/Healthcare)

```
A leading global provider of healthcare software solutions is looking for an experienced engineer to join the team in Hanover. This role focuses on developing advanced e-health applications that leverage artificial intelligence to improve medical workflows and patient care. The successful candidate will work across the full stack to integrate agentic AI experiences into a diverse portfolio of cloud-native products.

What you'll be doing

Designing and operating modern web applications across a comprehensive SaaS portfolio.

Integrating AI-native features and agentic experiences using LLMs, retrieval, and structured workflows.

Developing cloud-native services with a focus on reliability, security, and developer productivity.

Collaborating with product management and UX teams to establish best practices for AI engineering.

Building shared agents, skills, and pipelines while maintaining a strong focus on healthcare workflows.

What we're looking for

Extensive senior-level experience in full-stack engineering with React, Angular, Java, or Python.

Advanced degree (Master's or PhD) in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field.

Practical experience with cloud-native technologies including Kubernetes, Terraform, and Kafka.

Proven expertise in AI engineering, including experience with LLM tools and agentic coding workflows.

Professional fluency in both German and English with strong problem-solving and communication skills.

What's on offer

Opportunity to work in a dynamic and innovative environment within the health-tech sector.

Professional development in the rapidly evolving field of agentic AI and cloud services.

Collaborative culture focused on high engineering quality and outcome-driven development.

Role based in Hanover with a meaningful impact on the future of the healthcare system.
```
