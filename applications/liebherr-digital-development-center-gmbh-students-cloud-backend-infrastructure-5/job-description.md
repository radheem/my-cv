# Job Description — Student in Cloud Applications

```
We are looking for motivated students in the area of Cloud Applications for internship, thesis (Bachelor/Master) or as working student to support our software development team in Ulm.
You will participate in shaping our digital products we develop for various divisions within the Liebherr Group for which we provide production grade solutions like fleet management, control software for autonomous wheel loaders and apps for smart fridges.

Your responsibilities:
- Implement software solutions in the area of cloud backend and infrastructure
- Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams
- Be creative and adaptable, ready to tackle different tasks
- Embrace agile practices and methodologies

Your qualifications:
- Currently studying Computer Science or a related field
- First experiences in programming languages such as Java, C# or Go
- Basic knowledge in backend development (APIs, databases, etc.)
- Familiarity with cloud computing concepts and technologies, like MS Azure, AWS, GCP, containerization and Kubernetes
- Motivated and enthusiastic about working in an agile team environment
- Profound communication skills in both German and English
```
