# Job Description — Data Engineer

```
RS2 offers secure payment services, payment software and managed services to clients in over 35 countries. As a Data Engineer within the AI and Automation division, you will design, build, and maintain the data infrastructure that powers Business Intelligence and AI initiatives. Transform fragmented data sources into reliable, scalable, and actionable data assets.

Responsibilities: Data Pipeline Development - Design, build, and maintain robust ETL/ELT pipelines. Business Alignment & Impact - Partner with cross-functional teams. Data Architecture & Warehousing - Develop and maintain data models, warehouses, and storage solutions. Data Integration - Consolidate data from diverse sources (Jira, Confluence, source code repos, CRM, scheduling). AI/ML Readiness - Prepare structured datasets for analytics and ML. Data Quality & Governance - Implement data quality controls, monitoring, validation, lineage. Database Optimization. Continuous Improvement & Monitoring. Cross-Functional Problem Solving. DevOps & CI/CD. Documentation & Knowledge Sharing. Analytics & Reporting Support.

Requirements: BSc/MSc in Computer Science, Data Science, Analytics. Strong proficiency in Python. Strong SQL skills with PostgreSQL, MySQL, or Oracle. Experience with ETL/ELT pipelines, data warehousing, dimensional modelling. Experience with REST APIs. Experience with GitLab CI/CD. Strong analytical skills. Excellent communication skills in English.

Nice to have: Apache Airflow. dbt. Power BI or Tableau. AI/ML experience. Jira/Confluence. Docker.
```
