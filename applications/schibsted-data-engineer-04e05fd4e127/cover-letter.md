## 1. Why Schibsted Media?

A shared data platform works best when it quietly handles complexity so analytics teams can focus on insights rather than infrastructure. That balance between robust engineering and developer experience is what draws me to the Data Engineer role at Schibsted Media.

## 2. Why me?

I have spent the last five years designing data pipelines that move information reliably between systems. On a recent platform project, I built an automated ingestion workflow using Kafka that pulled information from multiple external sources and routed it through a verification step before it reached downstream databases. That verification step caught malformed records early and prevented corrupted data from reaching analytics dashboards. I also developed Python tooling that exposed clean, read-only data interfaces from a PostgreSQL database, which reduced friction when developers needed to access information. I also built automated quality gates that ran after every data refresh, which gave the team confidence that downstream reports were accurate. I enjoy writing clear documentation and participating in code reviews because they keep shared systems understandable for everyone who touches them.

## 3. Why now?

The opportunity to help shape a shared data platform that powers analytics and AI across a large media organization aligns perfectly with where I want to focus my career. I am eager to bring this focus on reliable data infrastructure to a team that values clean architecture and shared tooling. I am available to start full-time immediately and can relocate within Germany within two to three weeks. A Blue Card sponsorship is straightforward since the employer only needs to issue the employment contract. I look forward to discussing how my background in reliable data infrastructure can support the Data Infrastructure team.
