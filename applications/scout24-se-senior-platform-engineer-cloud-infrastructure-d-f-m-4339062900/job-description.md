As a Senior Platform Engineer in the Cloud Infrastructure in our Application Platform organization, you will shape, build and maintain a scalable, reliable and state-of-the-art compute platform for the Scout24 product development teams. Our platform supports various networking, persistence and compute requirements for all Scout24 applications and provides seamless capabilities and integrations for our internal developers. You will also get the opportunity to develop and integrate observability services that will help our engineers gain valuable insights into their applications.

Our motto is "You build it, you run it, you own it”. Your contribution will empower our teams to build products our customers love, enable them to make decisions based on data, and to run their applications with ease. As a senior member of the team, you lead by example by delivering outcomes over outputs. We are built on top of AWS (Amazon Web Services), as well as popular open-source solutions such as Kubernetes and AWS CDK (Cloud Development Kit). Our observability platform includes tools such as DataDog, OpsGenie, as well as our own ELK stack.

You can learn more about our compute platform on Scout24’s tech blog: https://medium.com/scout24-engineering/infinity-our-compute-platform-df4ec4538df5 .
