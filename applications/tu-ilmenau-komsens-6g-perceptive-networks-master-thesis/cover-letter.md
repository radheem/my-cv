## 1. Why Technical University of Ilmenau?

The future of manufacturing lies in the seamless, real-time synchronization between robotic control loops and perceptive communication networks. Having completed my research project at the Technical University of Ilmenau with a 1.0 grade, I am deeply inspired by the EMS department's pioneering work in software-defined instrumentation and integrated sensing. The KOMSENS-6G project's mission to digitize physical systems in real-time, specifically cooperative robots on the factory floor, is the exact challenge I want to address for my Master's Thesis. I am eager to apply my background in distributed systems and real-time telemetry to help build reliable, low-latency communication networks that function as physical sensors.
