## 1. Why Technical University of Ilmenau?

A good communication network does not just transmit data; it perceives and reacts to the physical environment to close the control loop. My academic journey at the Technical University of Ilmenau in the Master of Research program has shown me the incredible potential of merging 6G standards with real-time feedback systems, and I am highly motivated to pursue my Master's Thesis as a Research Assistant on the SENSATION project. Contributing to your group's work on distributed object tracking and drone detection allows me to combine my passion for real-time control theory with my hands-on background in cellular network platform engineering. This thesis represents the perfect step to transition my research from passive monitoring to active, closed-loop cyber-physical coordination.
