# Job Description — Working Student Engineer / Full-Stack Developer

```
VoiceLine is a fast-growing, VC-backed startup based in Munich. We build an AI-powered solution that helps field sales and service teams turn conversations into structured data efficiently, and helps them be smarter on the road. This enables customer information to be documented, analyzed, and seamlessly integrated into existing systems.

Responsibilities:
- Work full-stack with a focus on backend, building robust and scalable features across the entire product
- Design and implement AI-powered features that are core to our product, from prototype to production
- Own product features end to end — from initial concept and technical design through to delivery and iteration
- Act as the bridge between Product, Design, AI, and Customer Delivery teams, translating complex technical realities into clear, actionable communication
- Engage directly and ad hoc with customers to understand their needs and feed that insight back into the product

Requirements:
- Initial professional experience in a working student engineering role or multiple internships
- Hands-on experience implementing and working on AI features in a product context
- Strong interest in programming as a field
- Excellent written and spoken English; German is a plus
- Interest in UI/UX topics and willingness to contribute to frontend work when needed

Benefits:
- Dynamic environment with flexible working hours and strong team culture
- Competitive compensation package including virtual equity participation
- Modern office in Munich and flexibility to work remotely
- Access to Wellpass and sports/wellness offerings
- Support for public transport (Deutschlandticket)
```
