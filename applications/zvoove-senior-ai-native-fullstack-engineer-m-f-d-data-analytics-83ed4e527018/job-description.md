# Job Description — Fullstack Engineer (Data Focus)

```
We are building a modern analytics and Business Intelligence solution for customers in the temp-staffing industry, integrating operational data from multiple ERP systems across countries into reliable, customer-facing insights, analytical workflows, and reusable data products.

We are looking for a fullstack engineer with a strong data focus: someone who can move from messy ERP data and product-defined KPIs to validated datasets, pipelines, APIs, internal tools, and dashboards where needed. AI and LLM tooling are central to how we work.

Tasks: Fullstack Product Engineering (backend services, APIs, internal tools, UI/admin screens, automation, job runners, integrations). Data Pipeline & Modeling (ingest, validate, transform, document ERP, API, SQL, file, cloud data). Curated Data Products (validated, analysis-ready datasets). Cloud & Production Ownership (AWS, monitoring, alerts, failure handling, performance, cost).

Profile: AI-Native Development (Claude Code, Codex, agent-based workflows, MCP, structured prompts, harness engineering, parallelization). Strong fullstack/backend experience (Python and/or TypeScript). Data Engineering & Analytics (strong SQL, data modeling, analytical schemas). Cloud & Infrastructure (AWS, storage, databases, queues, containers, serverless). Benefits: Flexible working, hybrid/home office, modern tech environment, pension plans, health offers, employee discounts.
```
