# How to write a tailored cover letter

The per-job cover letter (`docs/jobs/<slug>/cover-letter.md`) is **~4 paragraphs,
~250-400 words**, half-to-one page. Written for a non-technical first reader (HR /
recruiter) — keep it light; the CV and portfolio carry the technical depth.

Source material: `data/profile.yml` (situation, narrative, logistics),
`data/master-cv.md` (facts), and the application's own `job-description.md`.

## Structure — introduction → body → closing
- **Introduction (1 paragraph).** State which role and a brief professional intro. Say why
  you want *this* specific position and organisation. Close with a short overview of the
  main strengths you bring.
- **Body (2-3 paragraphs).** Cite a couple of concrete examples from experience that prove
  you can succeed. **Complement the CV — don't repeat it in paragraph form.** Name the
  skills you developed and connect each back to the target role. Add specifics/numbers
  where you have them (the five-step ingestion pipeline, multi-vendor adaptor integrations,
  50+ Mbps, research 1.0/A).
- **Closing (1 paragraph).** Restate interest and fit; thank them. Put logistics here:
  availability and relocation.

## The opening line (hook)
- Light and attention-grabbing, not dramatic — this is a formal setting.
- Avoid the flat "I am writing to apply for…" and avoid an information-packed multi-clause
  first sentence. One clean, genuine idea.

## Rules
- **Tailor per company** — never send a generic letter; mention the specific role and a
  real "why".
- **Truthful** — no invented experience, skills, metrics, or dates.
- **One idea per sentence**; keep sentences short.
- Salutation: `Dear Hiring Team,` is fine; never "To Whom it May Concern".
- Lead with the narrative that fits the role: **deep system design / distributed systems**,
  **research rigor (top grades)**, or **build-from-scratch / ship-it** for startups.
