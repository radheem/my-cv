# Cover Letter — Platform Engineer, Northwind Cloud

Dear Hiring Team,

Running a multi-tenant developer platform is exactly the kind of problem I enjoy most, so the Platform Engineer role at Northwind Cloud was an easy one to get excited about. I am a platform and cloud-native engineer with five years across distributed systems and Kubernetes delivery, and I would bring hands-on cluster operations, GitOps-style delivery, and a focus on developer experience to your team.

Most of my recent work has been making Kubernetes clusters predictable and self-healing. In my HA K3s platform project I run clusters kube-proxy-free in eBPF mode with Cilium as the sole CNI, with L2 LoadBalancer announcements and a sequential-boot orchestrator for IP stability. In a companion homelab I made deploying a Service reachable by name with automatic HTTPS — ExternalDNS publishing into an authoritative CoreDNS, a shared Cilium Gateway terminating a cert-manager wildcard certificate. That maps closely to your need for cluster tooling, cloud-native networking, and DNS.

On the delivery and services side, I have owned release processes and containerization for production services as a senior engineer, and I build Go and Python services with gRPC and Protobuf-first APIs. I care about observability too — my projects wire up Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry so reliability work is grounded in real signals rather than guesses.

I am available and would welcome the chance to talk about how I can help Northwind Cloud's platform team. Thank you for your time and consideration.
